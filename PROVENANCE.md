# Scope and numerical provenance

This release is specific to *Cell composition and endpoint proximity constrain model-derived fate interpretations in three hematopoietic single-cell datasets*. `provenance/source_copies.json` records the original and released SHA-256 values of its allowlisted inputs and utilities. `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json` closes the supplied source inventory; it excludes its own recursive hash. These records bind software and aggregate data without including internal review or execution diaries.

It does not require full historical receipts. Its formatted outputs are checked against the supplied CSV/TeX references.
